  • The event Signal group: create it before the day. They opt in during onboarding as the announcements channel, and it's the room-only channel the day-two offer link (docs.denlennie.com/banana.html) goes out on.
  • Venue tech: Wi-Fi tested for eleven laptops on live AI, 4G hotspot as backup, power strips on every table, round tables, genius bar staffed (Arek plus one).
  • Growth Intelligence Reports read for the seven who completed the audit, so each person's placement (Elite-ready or Ascend) is set before day one.

The close, in detail

The room is mostly returning members and two from the original Elite group. This is a return to something more valuable, not a first-time pitch. No current full-rate Elite is present, so the room-only rate can be named openly, with "please keep this inside the room". Day one stays subtle: seed progression and support, build the trust, do not reveal the offer. The offer comes on day two. Lead with outcome, more time, less working, the weight off, not the mechanism.

  • The reveal (responsive, not pre-planned): "Overnight a couple of you asked me what happens after this, whether there's a way to keep going. So I've made a call. I'm reopening my UK Elite Boardroom, first time since 2022, and I'm starting with this room."
  • What it is: me in your corner every week, one to one on Signal, and I don't just advise, I build. Your offer, your tools, your site, your own AI OS. Everything in Elite. The in-person events aren't part of it for a UK room, they're welcome anytime and get every recording.
  • Two ways in: (1) The Elite Boardroom, no onboarding fee (the standard $6,800 waived), $1,295 a month locked for the life of the membership if they commit by Friday, about £975, which is $250 under the standard $1,549, no contract. (2) Ascend, $749 a month, cancel anytime, the group programme, fortnightly with you and alternate weeks with Caleb or Matt, the slower, lower-commitment path for anyone not ready for Elite.
  • The ROI line: most members make it back fast, and it's why people stay four and five years paying every month. The return keeps earning its place.
  • The why (the long game): waiving the onboarding is a real $6,800 off, over £5,000, before they pay a penny. Frame it as an investment, not a discount. They've already put in time, travel and hotels, and the room only scratches the surface. Working one to one accelerates results. You'll take the hit on the way in because if they're serious you'll help them get what they want. You're not here to fleece anyone up front, you're playing the long game, and the five-year members have doubled and tripled. Simple ROI, theirs and yours.
  • Ascend pro-rata: anyone already in Ascend who moves up has this month's payment credited across automatically. They won't pay for both.
  • The document: the offer is a separate, hidden page, live at docs.denlennie.com/banana.html (noindex, linked from nowhere) with the join buttons built in (Elite to the ThriveCart cart, Ascend to the denlennie.com/ascend page). Shown only at the day-two reveal, never before. Project it on screen as you talk to it, and drop the link into the event Signal group so it reaches the room and only the room.
  • The selectivity: this isn't for everyone in the room, and that's deliberate. I'm choosing who comes in. If you want to be considered, tell me and we'll talk.
  • The close: they join straight from the offer page, Elite via the ThriveCart cart and Ascend via the denlennie.com/ascend page. Signal is for questions and the nudge. The two settled weeks in Scotland are the enrolment window, so the soft close is safe, no momentum lost.

Show versus build: hold the line

Enough wins to feel capable, two big surfaces left visible and unfinished. That gap is the close. Don't over-deliver: an overwhelmed room retreats, and a finished room has no reason to join.

  • They build and own (the optimism): their scored Prospector list and its tracking, one Nurture message actually sent, one on-location content capture, their Claude Project hub, their 90-day plan.
  • Den shows (the hunger): SceneOps, the machine where leads live and get worked. The Offer Architect, current state to future state, the Elite tool. A taste of Claude Code, what's possible deeper.

The offer rebuild, the SceneOps setup, and going deeper are Boardroom work, on purpose. The 48-hour build is where the shown things get finished. That's what makes the Boardroom rate the obvious next step, not a pitch.
