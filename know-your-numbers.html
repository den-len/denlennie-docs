Why knowing your numbers lets you price with confidence

The owners who price with confidence do it because they know their numbers: their margin, their cost of delivery, what they actually pay themselves, the break-even under a quote.

Confidence is a numbers problem

Pricing anxiety is usually a numbers problem wearing a confidence mask. Owners who grind through their real margin, overheads and what the business needs to pay them can hold a price because it's a fact, not a feeling. The ones who avoid the numbers price from hope and fold the moment anyone leans on them. This is the foundation under the cost-of-delivery method: you can't build a price you trust if you don't know what your work actually costs.
