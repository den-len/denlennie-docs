11The oldest rule still standing

Right message. Right people. Right time.

When content stalls, it's almost never a volume problem. It's the message. The yap format didn't save me. Saying the thing my market loses sleep over did.

13The framework

Five beats hold the minute.

  1. Hook. Why should I care?
  2. Context. Where are we?
  3. Conflict. What went wrong?
  4. Turning point. What changed?
  5. Resolution. So what? And a question back.

A story structure, not a sales structure. That's why people stay.

15One theme, five videos

Five videos, one sitting, released across the week.

18Make it yours

Find your money theme. It's already in your inbox.

  1. What do clients ask me again and again?
  2. What do they misunderstand right before they buy?
  3. What conversation do I dread having, that they need to hear?

The answers are your first month of content.

19Exercise

Write your first hook. Right now.

[What everyone believes] is wrong. Here's what's true.
A client just [result, with a number]. Here's what changed.
There are two types of [your customers].
Stop [common behaviour]. It's costing you [specific cost].

Write three. Say one out loud. That's video one.

20This week

Phone out. Front camera. Sixty seconds. Post it.

Don't buy a microphone. Don't build a studio. The feeling of being ready arrives after the tenth video, not before the first.

The gear was never the barrier. And I say that as the video guy.
