How to get more from referrals and networking

Networking and referrals generate warm conversations, and most owners fail to follow through on them. Warm is a wasting asset.

The leak is the follow-through

The opens are easy: a room full of contacts, a client introduction, a dozen cards from an expo. The money leaks in the conversations left sitting for two weeks until they go cold. Owners chase cold outreach while warm introductions sit unanswered, because following up on a warm lead feels oddly more exposing, there's a relationship at stake. But a warm conversation you don't follow is worse than a cold one you never had, because it was yours to lose. Contact within days, and propose a real next step.
