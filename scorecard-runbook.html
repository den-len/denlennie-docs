Bring the assessment home.

The free S.C.A.L.E. Assessment is the site's main low-commitment entry point, and today it sends every finisher to Amazon or a legacy funnel on another domain. This runbook moves the five result pages onto denlennie.com and points every next step back at our own offers. Six steps, in order. Each step below is a standalone instruction sheet: open it, follow it top to bottom, tick the checks as you go.

Gates and rules

  • Order matters. Steps 01 and 02 must both be live before step 04 runs, or old links will redirect to pages that don't exist yet.
  • Step 05 is gated. Do not run it without Den's explicit go-ahead. It retires a live payment link.
  • Copy is final. Nobody rewords anything during the build, including the AI. If Lovable changes a word, make it restore the original.
  • If a "done when" check fails, stop. Message Den on Signal. Do not improvise a fix inside a live funnel.
  • House style for anything you type: no em dashes, no exclamation marks, British and Australian spelling.
