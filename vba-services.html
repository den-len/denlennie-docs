What VBA offers

VBA (Video Business Accelerator) is coaching, community and tools for video production company owners who want to grow without being in every seat. One programme, one shared toolset. For the price, see the pricing guide.

The VBA Elite Boardroom

The programme. Weekly Boardroom coaching calls with Den, a private community of peers who hold each other to account under a written code of conduct, and the full AI toolset. $1,700 USD a month, flat, cancel anytime, no application, no onboarding fee. Join at denlennie.com/elite-boardroom. Earlier programmes, including VBA Ascend, are closed to new members; existing members continue on their signed terms.

The VBA toolset

A set of proprietary AI tools built specifically for video production companies, not generic AI bolted on: a prospecting engine, a LinkedIn response engine, a Google Business Profile optimiser, service-page and blog-cluster builders, and schema markup. The principle is "less AI, used better": two tools used well beat ten used badly.

The method underneath

All the coaching runs on the S.C.A.L.E. Method, the framework from Den's 2026 book. It diagnoses where an owner actually is, from being stuck in every seat to leading and scaling, and routes the work to the one constraint that unlocks the next step.

Den Lennie is an author and business coach for video production company owners. He helps them escape the operator ceiling and build a business that scales without them.
