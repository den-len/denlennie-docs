What VBA offers

VBA (Video Business Accelerator) is coaching, community and tools for video production company owners who want to grow without being in every seat. Two ongoing programmes, one shared toolset, and your own marketing platform. For prices, see the pricing guide.

VBA Elite

The flagship programme, for established video business owners moving from operator to CEO. Coaching, a private community of peers who hold each other to account, the full AI toolset, and SceneOps. Application-only, because the standard of the room is the product.

VBA Ascend

For owners building from freelancer to genuine business owner. The same community and tools, coached by Den's team, focused on the earlier stages of the climb: pricing with confidence, a predictable pipeline, and the first systems that let the business run without you on every job.

The VBA toolset

A set of proprietary AI tools built specifically for video production companies, not generic AI bolted on: a prospecting engine, a LinkedIn response engine, a Google Business Profile optimiser, service-page and blog-cluster builders, and schema markup. The principle is "less AI, used better": two tools used well beat ten used badly.

SceneOps

Every Elite and Ascend member gets their own white-labelled SceneOps instance for marketing, pipeline and CRM, the same platform Den runs VBA on.

The method underneath

All the coaching runs on the S.C.A.L.E. Method, the framework from Den's 2026 book. It diagnoses where an owner actually is, from being stuck in every seat to leading and scaling, and routes the work to the one constraint that unlocks the next step.

Den Lennie is an author and business coach for video production company owners. Last reviewed 27 July 2026.
