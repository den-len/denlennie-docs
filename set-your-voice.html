Open the Nurture Engine

Do this first

Every reply and every post the Nurture Engine writes runs on your voice profile. Load it once and it powers the lot. Skip it and you get competent copy that reads like everyone else's.

Talk it, don't type it. Typing makes people formal. They write the website version of themselves, and the tool learns the wrong voice. Speaking catches how you actually put things, including the phrases you don't know you use.

How to do it

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone or laptop, and turn on voice mode (the headphones or microphone icon).
  2. Hit Copy the prompt below, and paste it in as your first message.
  3. Talk. It will interview you, one question at a time. Answer like you're chatting to a mate, tangents and all. That's where your real phrases live.
  4. When you've had enough, say "stop". It writes up your voice profile.
  5. Copy that profile and paste it into the My Voice tab in the Nurture Engine. From now on, everything you generate sounds like you.
The prompt 
We're going to do a short spoken interview so you can capture how I really talk about my work, and my posts and replies can sound like me, not like AI.

Interview me one question at a time, conversational, like a chat not a form. After each answer, ask one short follow-up if a better phrase or story is hiding in it. The good lines come in the tangents, so don't rush me.

Ask these one at a time:
1. What do you actually do? Say it like you would at a barbecue, not your website.
2. What do you always tell a nervous client?
3. What's the bit of your job people get wrong?
4. Tell me about a job that went well, and the moment it turned.
5. Tell me about a job that went sideways, and what you learned.
6. What's a phrase or analogy you use all the time?
7. What do you care about that most people in your trade don't?
8. Why do clients pick you over the cheaper option, in your words?

When I say stop, write up MY VOICE PROFILE under these headings, using my own words wherever you can: How I describe what I do; Phrases and analogies I use; Stories I tell; My honest take on my process; What I stand for and won't do. Give it to me as one clean block I can save and reuse.

Keep it current

Your voice profile is not a one-off. Come back to it when your positioning shifts, when you drop a service, or when you notice the output has started drifting away from how you sound. Run the interview again, replace what's in the My Voice tab, and everything downstream corrects itself.
