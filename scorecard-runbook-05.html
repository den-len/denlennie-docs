Scorecard funnel runbookSTEP 05

Retire the old Strategic Diagnostic.

Two different $595 diagnostics currently exist in public: the Growth Strategy Session on denlennie.com and the older "S.C.A.L.E. Strategic Diagnostic" at thescalemethodbook.com/strategic-deep-dive, which promises different deliverables and says "non-refundable". A prospect who finds both loses trust in each. After this step, only one exists.

Gated step. Get Den's explicit go-ahead before running this one. It retires a live payment link.
System GHL (thescalemethodbook.com) + SceneOps (Payments) Time About 15 minutes You need Step 04 completed · GHL admin · SceneOps payments access · Den's go-ahead

Steps

  1. Confirm with Den first that no one is mid-purchase on the old diagnostic (someone may have been sent the link privately). Do not skip this.
  2. In GHL Settings → Domains → URL Redirects (same place as Step 04), add: /strategic-deep-dive → https://denlennie.com/growth-strategy-session, permanent (301).
  3. Test in a private window: thescalemethodbook.com/strategic-deep-dive should land on the Growth Strategy Session page.
  4. In SceneOps, go to Payments → Payment Links and find the link ending …8d8161 (the old $595 Strategic Diagnostic). Deactivate or archive it. Do NOT touch the other $595 link ending …be374c; that one is the live Growth Strategy Session checkout.
  5. Open the deactivated link's URL in a private window and confirm it no longer accepts payment.
  6. Search for stragglers: if the old diagnostic is linked from any live email templates, automations, or the book's back-matter pages inside GHL, list what you find and send the list to Den. Do not edit emails or automations yourself.

Done when

  • Den gave the go-ahead before anything was touched.
  • /strategic-deep-dive redirects to the Growth Strategy Session page.
  • The old payment link is dead; the live GSS payment link still works (tested up to, not including, entering card details).
  • Any remaining references found in the straggler search are listed and sent to Den.

If something goes wrong

  • You deactivated the wrong payment link (the GSS checkout stops working): reactivate it immediately in SceneOps Payments and message Den. Test again before closing the session.
  • The redirect cannot be created: leave the old page live rather than deleting anything, and flag it. A live duplicate for another week is better than a broken link in the wild.
