What is the S.C.A.L.E. Method?

The S.C.A.L.E. Method is the framework from Den Lennie's 2026 book, and the engine underneath VBA coaching. It diagnoses which discipline is your current constraint and puts the work there.

The five disciplines

  • Survival. Focus on the right markets and projects.
  • Control. Nurture 50 to 100 high-value relationships.
  • Alignment. Position as a trusted partner, not a vendor.
  • Leverage. Build systems so you're not the bottleneck.
  • Expansion. Scale into bigger projects with consistent revenue.

Why it works

Most owners try to fix a structure problem with more effort. The point of the method is that at any moment one of the five disciplines is the thing actually holding you back, and progress comes from working that one, in order, rather than everything at once. Simplify first, systemise second, and never build complex systems on top of chaos.

Try this

Of the five, Survival, Control, Alignment, Leverage, Expansion, which is the one capping you right now? That's where the work is, and it's usually not the one you're spending your time on.
