The marketing asset you already have and keep ignoring

Before you make anything new, use what you've already got. The asset you keep ignoring is often the fastest win in the business.

Mine the archive first

The instinct is to create fresh content when the answer is usually sitting in existing work: footage you've already delivered, years of portfolio, the case you prepped for one client that transfers to every similar pitch. New creation feels like progress and reuse feels like cheating, so owners commission the next shoot instead of mining the last one. But you're sitting on delivered projects, results and prep that repackage into proof and outreach. Look at what you have before you decide what to build.

Try this

Before your next "I need to make something", list three things you've already delivered that you've never turned into a piece of proof or a reason for someone to call you.
