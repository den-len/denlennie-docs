Watch it for two weeks.

Ten minutes, twice a week, four checks total. Confirm the repatriated funnel keeps working in the wild and give Den early signal on whether result-page visitors click through to the Growth Strategy Session.

System A web browser + SceneOps Time 10 minutes, twice a week, for two weeks You need Steps 01–05 completed · the webhook tag/source noted in Step 03

Each check

  1. Quiz alive: in a private window, start the quiz at denlennie.com/scorecard, answer three questions, abandon. It should advance smoothly with no errors.
  2. Redirect alive: open thescalemethodbook.com/results-page-juggler and confirm it lands on denlennie.com/scorecard/juggler.
  3. Leads arriving: in SceneOps Contacts, filter for contacts created via the quiz webhook since the last check (use the tag/source noted in Step 03). Record the count.
  4. Clicks moving forward: if site analytics are available, note visits to the five /scorecard/* pages and to /growth-strategy-session for the period. If not available, skip; do not install anything.
