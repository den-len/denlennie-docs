How to use your own story to position your video business

Your own story is usually your strongest positioning asset, and most owners walk straight past it.

The wedge you're hiding

The background you already have, an industry you came from, a career before this, a place you're rooted in, is the thing that makes you the obvious choice for a specific kind of client. Someone who came from the trades wins construction work. Someone ex-military wins defence and aerospace. Someone who came home to a region wins the businesses there. Owners think positioning has to be invented or borrowed, but the thing that differentiates you is often the part of your history you dismiss as irrelevant. It signals you understand the client's world from the inside, which no competitor with a better reel can claim.
