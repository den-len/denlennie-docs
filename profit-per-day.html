Before you fill in the days

What counts as a day

This is where the number goes wrong, so read it first. A day is any day of your life the job took, not just the days you had a camera in your hand.

  • The enquiry call and the follow-ups
  • Writing the quote or proposal
  • Pre-production, scripting, planning, scheduling
  • Travel. Four hours each way is a day, not a detail
  • The shoot
  • The edit, including every round of revisions
  • Delivery, uploads and client hand-holding
  • Chasing the invoice

Count half days as 0.5. Client management and pre-production are the two that catch people out. When you are not sure, guess high, because everyone guesses low and a low denominator makes bad work look profitable.

Cost your own days too. Put yourself in the crew rates at a real rate. If you do not, every job looks profitable and none of them tell you anything.
