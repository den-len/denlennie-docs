The AI landscape
Six tools. One prompt can give you three different answers.
Claude
The work engine
Where we work over the two days. Writing in your voice, careful reasoning, building. The one to really learn.
ChatGPT
Your intern
The everyday all-rounder, and where most people live, in the chat window. Capable and quick, but it needs a clear brief.
Gemini
The Google layer
Strong on the web, YouTube and broad marketing research. Reach for it when the job touches Google's world.
Manus
Deep research
Hand it a brief and it works the problem over many steps, rather than answering in one turn.
NotebookLM
Big documents
Feed it a pile of source material, transcripts, reports, long PDFs, and it digests the lot: answers grounded in what you gave it, and the key nuggets pulled out. The one for making sense of a lot of copy.
Grok
Real time
What's happening right now, on X and across the web. The live pulse, less for everyday business writing.
One prompt, three answers, and they can all be right. The skill isn't picking a tool, it's knowing what's true, what's off, and what sounds like you. Guide it with what you know. Don't follow it blindly.
