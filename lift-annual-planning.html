Session four 05

Values: what we hire and fire on

We start from the moments, not from a list of nice words. Each person shares their "this is why I work here" moment and their "never again" moment. Then we ask: what value was being honoured in the proud ones, and violated in the never-agains?

Push past integrity, honesty and quality. Everyone writes those and nobody operates by them. "We don't ship anything we wouldn't show the client's CEO" beats "Excellence" every time.

Candidate values from the room

The test

Every candidate value has to pass both questions. If either answer is no, it's a preference, not a value. Cut it.

Would we hire someone because of this?
Would we part ways with a good performer who kept breaking it?

Our values: the final four to six

In this room's words. These go on the one page.

Session five 06

The number

We build the financial target for the coming year together, starting from what we know: last year's revenue, the shape of the client base, what's already contracted or likely. The question in the room: what's a number that would make us proud, and is believable from this room?

The annual number

The monthly number

Why monthly matters. The monthly number is the annual number divided by twelve. An annual number is abstract. A monthly number is a decision you make each Monday.

What has to be true for us to hit the monthly number

More ongoing partnerships, faster turnaround, fewer one-offs, something else. These feed straight into the chain.

Session six 07

The chain

This is the engine of the day. We take the strongest clusters from the look-back and the "what has to be true" list, and work each one left to right across a row. A complaint can't be ticked, measured or delegated. An action with a date can.

Accomplishment or lesson How do we... (the outcome we want) Given that... (the obstacle) So we will... (the action)
Our best clients keep coming back project after project How do we turn repeat projects into recurring revenue We've never formally offered ongoing partnerships to clients who already trust us Pitch our partnership model to our three warmest accounts, with a date on it

Parking lot

Good ideas that aren't this quarter's work. Parked without apology, not forgotten.

Session seven 08

Quarterly TOPs

From the chain, the room votes for the three or four actions that matter most in the next 90 days. Three dots each. Every TOP gets written as already done, in the present tense, with one owner and one date. "We have three partnership proposals in front of warm clients by 30 September" is a TOP. "Do more marketing" is not.
